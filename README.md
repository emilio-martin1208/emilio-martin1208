## About

I am a product engineer and indie builder interested in what happens when software leaves the demo and has to become dependable: the interface must make sense, the backend must survive real input, and an AI system has to admit what it does not know.

I work across product decisions, schemas, APIs, model behaviour, native UI, infrastructure, and the details that make a finished system feel considered. My interests include AI, cybersecurity, software, finance, games, and creative tools; the common thread is systems—how they behave, where they fail, and how design can make complexity feel obvious.

## Current work — Laneway

[Laneway](https://github.com/lanewayapp) is a Canada-first native iOS navigation product for journeys ordinary maps cannot fully see.

Mapping tools work when every connection exists in their graph. The hard case is the missing edge: an unindexed airport shuttle, terminal navigation, a hotel van, or an informal connection between otherwise valid routes. Laneway adds those edges without pretending certainty.

> **Never present a fact the system cannot back up.**

<img src="./assets/laneway-trust-loop.svg" width="100%" alt="A source-backed claim moving through Laneway's planning and verification system" />

Every factual claim carries its source and fetch time. Verification can return **supported**, **contradicted**, or **unsupported**; a partial route names the unknown rather than hiding it. The product separates slower reasoning from fast deterministic execution, keeping live data distinct from durable facts.

`Swift` `SwiftUI` `Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Redis` `Google Routes` `GTFS` `Anthropic`

## Selected work

### [Shinobi](https://github.com/emilio-martin1208/shinobi)

Shinobi turns one long-form video into vertical, subtitled clips. It transcribes, finds promising moments, follows faces, renders word-level captions, and produces platform-ready metadata. The interesting work is the media pipeline around the model: timing, crops, rendering, fallbacks, and making a suggestion into a publishable artifact.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Claude` `Whisper` `ffmpeg` `OpenCV`
