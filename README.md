## I Build the Whole Thing

I turn ambitious ideas into products people can actually use: shaping the system, writing the backend, building the interface, and chasing the last stubborn failure through production.

My favorite work sits where **AI, automation, and product engineering** meet. I care less about impressive demos than dependable systems with clear boundaries, grounded outputs, and a reason to exist.

Right now I am contributing to **verified trip routing** and building tools that make complex creative and engineering workflows feel simple.

```text
        ↓
Measure what failed and make it boringly reliable
```
